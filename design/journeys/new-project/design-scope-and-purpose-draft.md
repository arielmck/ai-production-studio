## Document Anti-Drift

Apart from this fixed section, this document contains exactly three sections, in order: a one-paragraph `Scope and Purpose`, a one-sentence `North Star`, and a one-to-three-paragraph agent-facing `Anti-Drift`; implementation instructions, code navigation, process history, and additional sections belong elsewhere.

Consult `document-standards/design-scope-and-purpose-document-standard.md` before formulating, proposing, or making changes; modifications to this file are prohibited unless the user explicitly requests changes to this specific file.

## Scope and Purpose

The New Project journey establishes everything needed to begin developing a project in support of its intended purpose. Through focused questions and creative proposals, it helps me make informed decisions about the foundation that best serves that purpose. Drawing upon the Studio's accumulated experience, it creates the project repository by adapting and extending the Project Template, establishes the appropriate technical foundation, and integrates the new project into my development environment.

## North Star

I can immediately begin developing my new project with a complete and effective foundation tailored to its purpose, fully integrated into my development environment, and thoughtfully prepared for foreseeable development needs.

## Anti-Drift

Do not expand New Project into Project Definition, Visual Presentation, application development or ongoing project management. Do not require the complete application design or all eventual engineering decisions to be settled before development begins.

Do not redefine the systems a new project joins. Do not modify the authoritative Project Template, Studio-wide rules or the development environment's own project as a side effect of creating a project. Do not duplicate Studio-wide rules within the new project or assume responsibility for updating existing projects when those systems evolve.

Do not mistake thorough preparation for speculative implementation. Anticipate foreseeable needs, distinguish what warrants establishment now from what is better addressed during later development, and preserve meaningful deferred decisions and needs in their appropriate owners. Do not impose unnecessary technologies, structures, documents or engineering practices merely because other projects use them, and do not take decisions reserved for me out of my control.
