# Evolving Practices

Project: AI Production Studio

An Evolving Practice is an evidence-informed area of working practice that is
expected to change as evidence, AI agents, tools, and the industry change. It
is not permanent policy, and it is not Deferred Work. It may stay provisional
for a long time, or indefinitely.

## Identity and naming

- `EP-###` is an Evolving Practice's stable identity. Its descriptive title may
  be renamed when understanding changes. Each EP has its own folder named by its
  ID, so a renamed title moves nothing.
- Name an EP for the enduring question or problem space, not for the current
  mechanism, artifact, proposed solution, or fashionable tool.
- The descriptive name is navigation, not scope authority. The enduring
  question owns the inquiry, and the name never constrains what later evidence
  is allowed to conclude. Do not accumulate material merely because it
  resembles the current title, and do not preserve a mechanism's importance
  after evidence shows it has become secondary.

## What an EP keeps apart

Documenting an idea must not silently make it binding, so an EP distinguishes:

- evidence;
- the current synthesis of what that evidence suggests;
- proposals and hypotheses;
- actual operating rules.

An actual operating rule is a `Current Standing Rule`. It is dated with its
effective date and is explicitly revisable rather than permanent truth.

Evidence and conclusions may contradict earlier evidence as capabilities
change. Preserve the current truthful synthesis rather than maintaining
obsolete conclusions for consistency.

## Keeping it light

Split an EP into additional files only when real accumulated content,
ownership, or readability makes that useful. There is no index, registry,
validator, or synchronization: the `EP-###` folders here are the list.
