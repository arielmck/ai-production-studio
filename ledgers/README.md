# Ledgers

## Document Anti-Drift

This README owns navigation to Studio-wide ledgers and identifies their respective responsibilities; it does not own their individual records, governing rules or editing permissions. Update it only through authorized work affecting those responsibilities or their navigation, without expanding a ledger's authority or duplicating its governing rules.

## Purpose

This directory contains Studio-wide ledgers that preserve and develop valuable information across Production Studio projects. Each ledger defines its own purpose, contents, authority and lifecycle.

## Best Ideas Ledger

`best-ideas-ledger.md` preserves our accepted Best Ideas and their value to the Production Studio, refined through experience across successive projects.

Project-specific Deferred Work and unreviewed Carryforward Best Ideas remain with their respective projects.
